Privacy Policy for GrowWell Diary

This privacy policy applies to the GrowWell Diary app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Jose M Toledo (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application obtains and stores information you provide directly within the app. Registration is not required to use the Application. The information you provide is used solely to provide the features of the Application and enhance your user experience.

This information includes, but is not limited to:

  • Plant Data: Plant names, photos, acquisition dates, and care details.
  • Species Data: Custom species names, care instructions, and other details you provide.
  • Planter Data: Details about your planters.
  • Journal Data: Dates, notes, and photos related to your journal entries.

This data is stored locally on your device.

iCloud Syncing

To provide a seamless experience across your devices, the Application uses Apple's iCloud service to sync your data. All of your Application data is stored securely in your private iCloud account. The Service Provider does not have access to this data.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

The Service Provider does not share your information with any third parties. Your data is stored either locally on your device or in your private iCloud account, as described above.

What are my opt-out rights?

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network. You can also disable iCloud syncing for the Application in your device's Settings.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Your data is protected by the security of your device and, if you enable iCloud sync, by the security measures of your personal iCloud account provided by Apple.
