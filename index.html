About Me

I'm a strategic cybersecurity advisor at Mandiant, part of Google Cloud, where I specialize in securing artificial intelligence deployments and bridging the gap between technical risk and enterprise business objectives. Operating across complex IT, OT, and AI landscapes, I have over a decade of experience moving cybersecurity from abstract theory to frontline execution.

With a dynamic background spanning enterprise network administration, cloud threat assessments, and executive tabletop simulations, I focus on translating modern, complex threat vectors into high-impact governance strategies. My work spans the entire risk lifecycle — helping global organizations safely adopt autonomous technologies and implement resilient guardrails that protect critical data assets.

Beyond my role at Google, I leverage cross-industry expertise to serve on corporate advisory boards, providing strategic counsel to organizations navigating the rapid evolution of emerging technologies.
